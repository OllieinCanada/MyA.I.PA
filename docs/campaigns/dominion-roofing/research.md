# Dominion Roofing campaign research

Research date: 2026-09-02

Only Dominion Roofing's official public website was used for company-specific claims. The campaign does not use Dominion's logo, photography, testimonials, customer records, or a company email address.

## Verified observations

- Dominion describes itself as a full-service Toronto roofing company serving residential and commercial customers across the GTA.
- Its public service navigation includes inspections, repairs, replacement, shingles, flat roofing, specialty roofing, maintenance, new construction, and related exterior work.
- The website advertises 24/7 support, free inspections/estimates, and fast emergency response.
- Its appointment flow asks for contact information, address, property type, service type, requested timing, and additional comments. The page states that requested appointment times are not guaranteed until the team confirms them.
- Dominion's public service areas include Toronto, North York, Etobicoke, Scarborough, Mississauga, Brampton, Burlington, Oakville, Markham, Richmond Hill, Vaughan, and other surrounding communities.

## Campaign strategy derived from those facts

The strongest non-invasive angle is overflow coverage behind Dominion's existing team: catch a call after three unanswered rings, gather the same kind of initial property and roofing context Dominion already requests, then create a callback-ready handoff. The campaign deliberately does not say My AI PA diagnoses a roof, quotes a price, books an appointment, or replaces Dominion's 24/7 team.
