# Canadian Counsel Review Handoff

Prepared: July 23, 2026
Status: draft package; no legal opinion or approval has been obtained

## Product summary

My AI PA provides AI-assisted phone answering for trade/service businesses, call intake, optional recording/transcription, lead summaries, owner and caller texts, dashboards, trial/subscription billing, support reporting, and optional calendar/field-service integrations. Core providers expected in production include Render/Postgres, Vapi, Twilio, OpenAI, Make, Stripe, GitHub Pages, and selected email/monitoring services.

## Facts counsel still needs from the operator

1. Full legal entity, form, registration jurisdiction/number, trade name, and mailing address.
2. Launch provinces and whether any US callers/customers are permitted.
3. Business-only versus consumer/sole-proprietor contracting.
4. Privacy Officer appointment and contact.
5. Insurance policies and limits.
6. Final plan price, currency, taxes, included usage, overages, trial end, card authorization, cancellation, refund, and number-offboarding rules.
7. Support hours, outage commitments, and any service-level promises.
8. Final provider agreements, processing locations, and data-retention/deletion settings.

## Requested opinions and approvals

- Applicable PIPEDA, provincial privacy, Quebec, recording, consumer-protection, accessibility, telecommunications, and AI-disclosure requirements.
- Approved AI and recording/transcription opening for every launch jurisdiction and an alternative when consent is refused.
- CASL classification of owner alerts, requested caller confirmations, trial reminders, support messages, and marketing.
- Privacy Policy, Terms, DPA, customer acknowledgement, subprocessor schedule, and security schedule.
- Retention periods, access/correction/deletion process, limitation/legal-hold rules, and privacy complaint language.
- Trial, renewal, cancellation, refund, usage-charge, and payment authorization language.
- Liability, indemnity, warranty, governing-law, venue, and insurance alignment.
- Marketing substantiation and removal of any unsupported “PIPEDA compliant,” customer-count, savings, performance, or testimonial claim.
